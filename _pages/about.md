Hi!

I’m a computer vision researcher and engineer at Woven by Toyota in Tokyo, originally from Sydney, Australia. I work on 3D vision, generative models, and neural rendering approaches.

I’m particularly interested in taking research all the way from an initial idea to something that actually solves real world problems.

Much of my recent work has focused on generative world models for physical environments, exploring spatially consistent video generation and action-conditioned modelling. Previously, I worked on reconstructing photorealistic 3D humans and their environments, including avatar reconstruction, neural rendering, 3D reconstruction and real-time tracking.

Feel free to reach out on LinkedIn or GitHub if there's anything you'd like to discuss!

**Research interests:** World models · 3D vision · Generative models · Neural rendering · Self-supervised learning · Representation learning · Efficient inference
